The eleventh of November was the busiest day of the month so far, with over a hundred pull requests merged across Skywire, Skycoin and the website. Most of them port web interfaces to server rendered pages built with templ and htmx. The hypervisor's new interface at `/ui2` gained nearly every page of the Angular one, the Skycoin wallet's did the same, the explorer switched over and became the default, and the skydex client started its own port. Underneath that, the router kept tidying its multipath legs, dmsg learned to say why a stream was refused, and the Android app took a long run of fixes from testers.

### Skywire: The Hypervisor at /ui2

**`6135`** starts with a parity table that lists every page, tab and action of the Angular hypervisor interface against what `/ui2` has, and each port after it fills one area and updates the table. The visor list gained labels, removal of offline visors, filters and sorting in **`6136`**, then row selection with bulk restart, reward address and app actions in **`6139`**, which go through the same handlers as the Angular dialogs. **`6141`** adds the settings page with password change and labels, and both interfaces keep their browser side settings under the same keys, so they agree. **`6142`** brings the app catalog and a flags form checked against the autoconfig value rules, and **`6161`** the apps list's filter and bulk start, stop and autostart.

Whole pages followed. **`6144`** adds deployment service health and the route setup node stats, **`6145`** every visor's host resources, **`6147`** the uptime of every visor, **`6156`** the network view of over a thousand visors, **`6157`** the network wide transport metrics, and **`6158`** a rewards view of the managed visors. **`6151`** adds per transport bandwidth for a visor, paged because a busy visor holds thousands of transports, and **`6159`** transport labels and details. **`6163`** adds the history charts for resources and versions.

The app tabs came over one by one. **`6150`** replaces the chat tab's embedded Angular page with a native one for direct messages and rooms, and **`6165`** adds the skychat password and room states. **`6153`** adds the web proxy tab, **`6154`** the skynet tab with forwarded ports and reverse proxies, **`6155`** the VPN tab and **`6164`** its server picker, **`6162`** the offline alert settings, and **`6148`** frames the Skycoin wallet's own `/ui2` in the wallet tab. Each port was checked against the development hypervisor, read only where a write would have disturbed a running visor, with handler tests covering the writes. **`6140`** fixed a lint finding from the list work.

### Skycoin: The Wallet, the Explorer and skydex

It gained a history page for every wallet in **`skycoin#3253`**, wallet options for node wallets in **`skycoin#3254`**, settings, backup, outputs and pending pages in **`skycoin#3255`**, and password reset, temporary loading and seed passphrases in **`skycoin#3257`**. **`skycoin#3256`** fixed the API underneath, which dropped the seed passphrase when loading a temporary wallet, so a passphrase wallet came up as a different empty one in both interfaces.

Unsigned and joint transactions arrived in **`skycoin#3258`**, sends to several destinations, from chosen sources and in bulk in **`skycoin#3259`**, and QR codes and payment links in **`skycoin#3260`**. Wallets kept in the browser caught up through **`skycoin#3261`**, **`skycoin#3264`** and **`skycoin#3265`**, which sign in the page and never post anything secret. **`skycoin#3262`** adds the node warnings under the top bar, **`skycoin#3263`** BIP44 accounts, **`skycoin#3266`** a cached history for when the node is down, **`skycoin#3267`** QR scanning from the camera or an image, and **`skycoin#3268`** the multi-coin pages for Bitcoin, Ethereum and ERC-20 tokens. The explorer was an Angular app, so search engines and readers without JavaScript got an empty page. **`skycoin#3248`** and **`skycoin#3250`** render every page on the server, each with a title, description and canonical link, and **`skycoin#3251`** makes that the default at the old URLs, with the Angular explorer kept at `/classic/` for one release. Along the way the address page learned to show whether an address has sent coins in **`skycoin#3245`**, which lets reward operators check their address, and the rich list now pages through every address with its decimal points lined up in **`skycoin#3246`**. **`skycoin#3247`** pages the rich list on the node itself, built once per block instead of on every request, and **`skycoin#3249`** updated the end to end specs.

The skydex client began its port in **`skycoin#3269`** and finished it the same day with the market tab in **`skycoin#3270`**, orders and listings in **`skycoin#3271`** and history in **`skycoin#3273`**, with a formatting fix in **`skycoin#3272`**, so every React tab now has a `/ui2` counterpart. **`skycoin#3274`** closes a cross-site hole in its control API, where a page visited while the client ran could buy, list or cancel through it. **`6129`**, **`6131`**, **`6132`**, **`6138`**, **`6152`**, **`6160`**, **`6167`** and **`6171`** brought all of this into Skywire.

### Skywire: Mux Legs and Range Requests

The leg refactor from the day before reached its third stage. **`6116`** keeps a mux leg's counters, readiness, standby flag and bottleneck group in one record with its transport ID, in place of four lists kept in step by hand. Traffic was credited through an index taken before the write, so a removal during the write credited the bytes to another leg, and that is gone with it. **`6117`** drops a leg's park time, delay estimate and latency when it leaves, where before a later leg on the same transport inherited them. **`6111`** tells the peer when a parked leg becomes the primary, so both ends agree on which legs are active.

Two fixes went into the proxy's range splitting, which fetches parts of one download over several tunnels. **`6114`** retries a chunk at once when its tunnel connection fails a write, where a narrow window in yamux had charged it a retry and a backoff. **`6119`** keeps one route from carrying more than its share of an object when no tunnel was eligible, which had let a tunnel over its cap win while an unmeasured standby sat out.

### Skywire: dmsg Refusals and a Parked Frame

A dmsg dial to a key with no session, or to a port with no listener, used to fail with a bare EOF that looked the same as a dropped connection. **`6113`** has the server answer its own client's refused request with the dmsg error code, signed with the server's key, and **`6115`** has the destination answer a request for a port it does not listen on with error 306, signed with its own key, which a forwarding server passes on unchanged. **`6122`** fixed a refusal test that raced the server listing its sessions.

A hypervisor can leave changes for an offline visor as recorded RPC frames, checked against an allow list when the visor comes back. The visor checked only the first request in a frame, so a second request in the same frame was served unchecked. Only a configured hypervisor can leave a change, so this bypassed the allow list and nothing more, and **`6112`** now requires exactly one request per frame.

### Skywire Mobile

Testers' reports kept the Android app busy. **`6123`** keeps a full open chat at its newest message, **`6124`** switches tabs at once on a tap and slides only on a swipe, **`6125`** counts the SkyChat badge the way the chat rows do, and **`6126`** translates the unanswered call and join request texts. **`6127`** builds a group backup in the cache and checks it before copying it out, after the first tries came out empty or corrupt, and **`6130`** clears a mail notification once the mail is read. **`6166`** finds the wallet addresses that the reward system pays past the ones a wallet already holds, and **`6170`** says when the phone has no internet while the core starts, instead of spinning on Starting.

SkyMail dates now show in the phone's time zone through **`6168`**, the compose screen shows the sent size of attachments against the limit in **`6169`**, and **`6176`** gives a mail send ten minutes, after a 7 MB attachment failed at the two minute bound every mailbox call used. **`6172`** stops the SkyVPN screen from restarting a running tunnel, and **`6173`** and **`6174`** try the exported tether settings activity for the hotspot button, which was not verified on the reporters' phones. On the visor side, **`6110`** checks `/data` for storage on Android, where the read-only system image had raised false warnings, **`6109`** reuses skychat's saved proxy token so a password protected chat page survives a restart, and **`6128`** scans groups for role and roster changes every second, which cut the delay a member saw from about 16 seconds to 4. **`6134`** adds the changelog for Skywire v1.3.110, released that afternoon, and the notes for Skywire Mobile 0.0.11.

### Skywire: CI, Rewards and the Website

Continuous integration got faster. **`6133`** keeps the Go build caches inside the repository's cache quota, after the caches grew on every push until GitHub evicted one lane's and its tests ran uncached. **`6143`** stops a deployment host from waiting at boot for services it hosts itself, which cost a minute, **`6146`** drops two fixed waits that made up most of the restart test's 170 seconds, and **`6149`** compiles the end to end binaries on the runner with a warm cache. **`6175`** fixed a double close in transports that panicked the router tests.

On the reward side, **`6137`** fetches uptime data over dmsg in the reward cycle, and **`6118`**, **`6120`** and **`6121`** repaired dead links in the rules and history pages. On skycoin.com, **`skycoin.com#89`** links the Skywire v1.3.109 release on the downloads page, **`skycoin.com#88`** adds the notice that Skycoin is unrelated to the Maker token renamed SKY, and **`skycoin.com#87`** fixes an image and two links in the CX pages.
