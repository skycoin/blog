### Step 1: Add a wallet

Make sure you have added a Skycoin wallet or loaded one from a seed already.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/1-1.png "Add a wallet")

### Step 2: Buy Skycoin

Access the new service for buying Skycoin, by simply clicking on the new “Buy Skycoin” button on the right of the navigation bar.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/2-1.png "Buy Skycoin")

<br>

### Step 3: Exchange details

You will then be shown a form with details of the exchange.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/3-1.png "Exchange details")

<br>

### Step 4: Select the deposit currency

Select the cryptocurrency you wish to deposit in exchange for Skycoin. You can choose from Ethereum, Monero, Bitcoin, Eos, and Litecoin.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/4-1.png "Select the deposit currency")

<br>

### Step 5: Enter the deposit amount

Enter the amount of coins that you’ll be depositing for the chosen cryptocurrency.

This will update the exchange information on the right-hand side, where you’ll see an approximation of the amount of Skycoin you’ll receive in return, based on the current exchange rate.

Please note that the exchange’s commission is set to 0.5% of the total amount of the transaction, and that the funds are typically sent within 15 minutes after the deposit is confirmed, depending on network conditions.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/5-1.png "Enter the deposit amount")

Please bear in mind that there is a minimum and maximum limit on how many coins you can send for the deposit, based on limits set by Swaplab. There will be a warning message that will indicate the limits if you exceed them.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/6-1.png "Minimum")

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/7-1.png "Maximum")

### Step 6: Select the receiving Skycoin address

Select the Skycoin address that you want to receive the Skycoin, by choosing one of your wallet addresses from the dropdown.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/8-1.png "Select the receiving Skycoin address")

You also have the option to manually enter a Skycoin address.

<br>

### Step 7: Accept the Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy to enable the exchange.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/9-1.png "Accept the Terms of Use & Privacy Policy")

<br>

### Step 8: Start the exchange

Start the exchange when you’re satisfied with all of the details by clicking the “Exchange” button.

<br>

### Step 9: Send the deposit

The next screen indicates the status of the exchange process.

The status will first indicate that the exchange is waiting for you to send your deposit to the exchange address mentioned.

The blue bar shows the progress of the exchange process, and in case you want to start another exchange, click the "Back" button to return to the previous form.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/10-1.png "Send the deposit")

You can also view more details by clicking the “Details” button.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/11-1.png "View details")

<br>

### Step 10: Wait for transaction confirmation

The exchange process will start after the deposit is detected and confirmed in the blockchain. If you have already sent the deposit, it should be detected shortly.

The status will update to indicate the process has started and that the exchange is now waiting for the transaction to be confirmed.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/12-1.png "Wait for transaction confirmation")

<br>

### Step 11: Transaction accepted

Once the transaction has been confirmed in the blockchain, the status updates to “Transaction accepted”, and the trade will soon be made.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/13-1.png "Transaction accepted")

<br>

### Step 12: Trade made

The exchange then makes the appropriate trade, and updates the status accordingly. The funds will soon be transferred to the receiving Skycoin address.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/14-1.png "Trade made")

<br>

### Step 13: Exchange sends the Skycoin

The exchange then initiates the process to send the Skycoin to the receiving address, with an appropriate status update.

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/15-1.png "Exchange sends the Skycoin")

<br>

### Step 14: Exchange complete

Once the Skycoin have been successfully sent to the receiving address, the status updates and the exchange is complete!

![Buy Skycoin with Desktop Wallet](/img/16-2.png "Exchange complete")

<br>
